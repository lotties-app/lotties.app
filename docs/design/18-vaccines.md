# Vaccinations: the vaccines a baby took and the schedule of vaccines due by age (design, 2026-10-22)

Owner request: record the vaccines the baby took and show which vaccines are due by age, from the Brazilian public schedule (PNI) and, when the family wants it, the private-clinic recommendations of SBP and SBIm. The schedule data and its research are in docs/design/17-vaccine-schedule-data.md (sources, disagreements, the 23 doses flagged for review, schema v2).

## 0. Guardrails
- The app is not medical advice. It never tells a parent to give or to skip a vaccine. Wording is "recommended", "due according to this schedule", "Confirm this with your pediatrician"; an expired dose reads "No longer in the schedule: ask your pediatrician" in a calm style (never an error colour); overdue is a neutral warning chip.
- Disagreements are kept, not resolved: alternatives of one slot (PNI vs SBP/SBIm products and ages) are shown side by side with the "Confirm this with your pediatrician" marker; the 23 `needsReview` doses carry the same marker.
- A private-clinic dose is never presented as offered by the SUS: with the public profile it is listed only under the Private filter, as "Not in the public schedule".
- Premature babies get a persistent notice ("Premature babies may follow a special schedule. Follow your pediatrician's guidance.") and the dates are not changed. Special populations are not covered (section 9).
- No personal data leaves the device for the vaccination card view (browser print / Save as PDF only). Push payloads carry codes only (section 6).

## 1. Schedule data (one source of truth)
- `code/api/data/vaccine-schedule.br.json` (schema v2, document 17 section 11) is embedded in `Lotties.Application` (`EmbeddedResource` with `WithCulture="false"`: `.br` is Brazil, not the Breton culture) and copied into the API image (`code/api/Dockerfile` copies `data/`). `EmbeddedVaccineScheduleProvider` parses it (`VaccineScheduleJson`, strict shape) and validates it (`VaccineScheduleValidator`) once; mapping the endpoints resolves it, so an invalid file stops the API at startup. The same rules run in `VaccineScheduleDataTests`.
- Validation: schema version 2; unique source ids, vaccine keys and dose ids (lower-case slugs, keys 40, dose ids 60); every reference resolves (vaccine keys, source ids, `previousDoseIds`, `covers`, never a record-only dose or the dose itself); `earliest <= recommended <= latest`; positive intervals, an interval needs `previousDoseIds`; `network` public exactly when a public source lists the dose; en and pt-BR for names, labels and notes; `needsReview` exactly when a reason is given; known `condition`s; a recurrence starts at the recommended age; a record-only dose covers something and has no group or recurrence; alternative groups have 2+ members, all recurring or none.
- `GET /api/v1/vaccine-schedule` (any signed-in user): the file bytes as shipped, `ETag: "br-{generatedOn}-{first 8 hex of SHA-256}"`, `Cache-Control: private, max-age=3600`, 304 without a body on a matching `If-None-Match` (CORS exposes `ETag`). The web keeps it in Dexie (`vaccineSchedules`, keyed by profile, with the etag) and works offline from that copy; it revalidates at most once an hour; an unknown schema version is ignored (the cached copy stays).

## 2. Baby profile
- Three baby columns (migration `Vaccines`): `vaccine_profile` text NOT NULL DEFAULT 'public' (`public` = SUS/PNI only, `public+private` = also SBP/SBIm), `gestational_age_weeks` smallint NULL (20-44; under 37 = premature notice), `rsv_maternal_vaccination` text NULL (`unknown`|`yes`|`no`; `null` = not answered, treated as unknown).
- `BabyResponse` carries `vaccineProfile`, `gestationalAgeWeeks`, `rsvMaternalVaccination` (additive). `PUT /babies/{id}/vaccine-profile` (Owner, Parent) `{vaccineProfile, gestationalAgeWeeks?, rsvMaternalVaccination?}` replaces all three (400 `validation_failed` with `field`); the birth date keeps its Owner-only edit flow.

## 3. Status engine (pure; C# `Lotties.Domain.Vaccinations.VaccineStatusCalculator`, TS `code/web/src/vaccines/vaccineStatus.ts`)
Inputs: schedule, profile, birth date, the records that are not deleted, today (all local calendar dates). Ages are whole days between dates (`DateOnly` day numbers; UTC day numbers in TS), so daylight saving and the time of day never move a date. Output per scheduled dose (record-only products left out), plus `ageDays` and `prematureNotice`.
1. A record satisfies its own dose and every dose that dose `covers` (a recorded hexavalente also satisfies `vip-1` and `hepatite-b-2`). Records without a dose id, or with an id the schedule no longer has, are ignored by the engine (still listed in the history). Records are ordered by date, then id text.
2. `given`: a satisfying record (the earliest; `coveredBy` names the combined product's dose when a covers rule applied). Given wins over every rule below, so a private dose recorded with the public profile still shows as given.
3. `notApplicable`: `alternative` (another dose of its alternative group was given; `alternativeOf`), `condition` (the RSV antibody when the mother got the RSV vaccine), `network` (a private dose with the public profile).
4. Window: `dueOn = birth + recommended`, `earliestOn = birth + earliest`, both pushed to `previous given + minIntervalDaysFromPrevious` when the latest given dose of `previousDoseIds` was late; `latestOn = birth + latest`.
5. `expired` after `latestOn`, or when the push leaves no valid day (`earliestOn > latestOn`); else `upcoming` before `dueOn`; `overdue` from `dueOn + 31` days (`overdueFrom`); else `due`.
6. Yearly doses (`recurrence {everyDays, startAgeDays}`): due from the start age, then `everyDays` after the latest dose of its series (itself, its group, or a `previousDoseIds` dose such as the first-season influenza doses). Given within `everyDays` of the latest record (`nextDueOn`); a sibling of its group given this season makes it `notApplicable`/`alternative`; while none of the series was given and a previous dose is still pending it waits (`upcoming`, `dueOn` null, `waitingFor`); never overdue (it stays due until given or past `latestOn`).
7. `optionalPrivate` marks private doses shown because the profile includes them.
Shared vectors `code/web/src/vaccines/vaccineStatusVectors.json` (31 cases: birth day, two months, the 30/31-day overdue edge, a late dose pushing the next one, combined covers, covered dose as previous, alternatives, public profile, a recorded private dose, RSV condition yes/no/unknown/unanswered, expired and pushed-past-latest, yearly waiting/due/given/again/alternatives/after the first doses expired, premature notice with the same dates, 37 weeks, leap-day birth, daylight saving, records outside the schedule) are read by `VaccineStatusVectorTests` (C#, linked file) and `vaccineStatus.test.ts` (also run under other `TZ`).

## 4. Entity `Vaccination` (per baby, synced like prescriptions)
- Fields: `id` (client GUID), `babyId`, `vaccineKey?` (slug 40), `doseId?` (slug 60, only with a vaccine key), `customName?` (1-80, required exactly when there is no vaccine key), `doseLabel?` (80, the label shown when recorded, in the recorder's language: history survives schedule updates), `givenOn` (2000-01-01 up to the server's UTC date + 1 day: never in the future for any time zone), `network` (`public`|`private`, where it was given), `clinic?` (120), `lot?` (40), `administeredBy?` (80), `notes?` (1000), `createdBy`, `createdAt`, `updatedAt` (server receive time, the delta cursor), `clientUpdatedAt` (client edit time, last write wins), `deletedAt`. Text trimmed, NUL rejected.
- `GET /babies/{id}/vaccinations?since=` (every member, Caregiver, Viewer and Doctor included): deleted included; with `since` by `(updatedAt, id)`, else by `(givenOn, id)`. `PUT /vaccinations/{vaccinationId}` (Owner, Parent): full state `{vaccineKey?, doseId?, customName?, doseLabel?, givenOn, network, clinic?, lot?, administeredBy?, notes?, updatedAt?}`, 201 + `Location` / 200; idempotent replay; stale client time ignored; a deleted record stays deleted (200, stored copy); another baby's id 409 `vaccination_baby_mismatch`. `DELETE /vaccinations/{vaccinationId}` (Owner, Parent): soft delete, always wins, idempotent, unknown id 204.
- Table `vaccinations` with indexes `(baby_id, given_on)` and `(baby_id, updated_at)`, FKs to the baby and the creator (restrict).

## 5. Web
- Dexie version 8: `vaccinations` (`id, babyId, [babyId+givenOn], [babyId+updatedAt]`) and `vaccineSchedules`. `pullVaccinations` after appointments (own cursor; a 404 from an older server is ignored), outbox `entityType: 'vaccination'` with upsert and delete (Undo re-records under a new id, as prescriptions), merge by client edit time, purge with the baby. Commands in `db/vaccinationCommands.ts` mirror the API rules.
- Route `/vaccines` (Settings > Vaccines, the "Vaccines due" card on Now when a dose is due or overdue: count per slot and the dose waiting longest, the push click target `/vaccines#notify=vaccine&baby=..`) and `/vaccines/card`.
- Page: "About this baby" (birth date; when missing, a state that opens the baby edit flow; "Born before 37 weeks?" with the weeks; "Did the mother get the RSV vaccine in pregnancy?" Yes/No/Don't know; "Show private clinic recommendations"; one "Save changes", online only, Owner/Parent), the premature notice, filters All | Public (SUS) | Private, the timeline grouped by recommended age ("At birth", months, years), cards with the vaccine name and dose label from the data, protects-against line, status chip (Given on date, "with <product>" when covered; Due now; Due in N days / Due on date; Overdue with "Recommended at <age>"; Not in the public schedule; No longer in the schedule: ask your pediatrician; Yearly / Next on date / After the earlier doses), source badges PNI/SBP/SBIm, a "Private clinic" marker and the "Confirm this with your pediatrician" marker (needsReview and visible alternatives). Doses not applicable by alternative or condition are hidden. Actions for writers: Record (sheet with that dose), Schedule it (opens the appointment sheet prefilled: type vaccine, title "Vaccine: <name>", the due day if ahead, else today; saved only on Save), edit/delete a record; a History list of every record (custom ones and doses no longer in the schedule included, shown by their snapshot). Footer "Sources" sheet (the 3 sources with edition, retrieval date and link, and "This schedule is informational. Follow your pediatrician and your vaccination card.").
- Record sheet (`VaccinationSheet.tsx`): vaccine (schedule vaccines by name, or "Other vaccine" with a required name), the dose it satisfies (default: the next pending dose of that vaccine; for combined products their record-only doses, the first whose covered doses are not all given; or "Not in the schedule"), date (max today), Public (SUS) / Private clinic (follows the chosen dose's network until touched), clinic, lot, administered by, notes.
- Vaccination card (`/vaccines/card`): baby name, birth date, table Date / Vaccine / Dose / Lot / Clinic (network) of the records that are not deleted, "Print / Save as PDF" (`window.print()`) and a print stylesheet (app chrome and buttons hidden, black on white).
- Roles: Caregiver, Viewer and Doctor read everything with no write buttons. Settings > Notifications per baby: "Vaccine reminders" (off by default; not offered to Caregivers, Viewers and Doctors).

## 6. Push `vaccine_due` (docs/design/09-push-notifications.md section 2.11)
- Preference `vaccine_reminders` (per member and baby, NOT NULL DEFAULT FALSE; PUT merge), Owners and Parents only, with the device opt-in. The candidate index filter gains `OR vaccine_reminders`.
- Planner: the statuses on the recipient's local date with the baby's profile. Per slot (an alternative group counts once; the member that became due first, schedule order on a tie) with a dose due or overdue (not given, not covered, not expired): the first reminder at 09:00 local on the day the slot became due, one follow-up 14 days later at 09:00 if still not given (at most 2 per dose). Inside quiet hours it moves to their end when that is the same local day, else that reminder is skipped. Only today's and yesterday's reminders are considered, so turning the switch on never sends old ones. Key `vaccine:{baby:N}:{doseId}:{n}` (a yearly dose adds its due date: `{doseId}@{yyyyMMdd}`); stale cutoff 12 h; TTL 48 h; urgency normal; no snooze.
- Payload `{v, id, kind: "vaccine_due", babyId, doseId, at (the send instant), lang, token?, names?: {baby, vaccine?}}`; `names.vaccine` is the vaccine's short name (before any parenthesis) in the device language, only with show-names. Lock screen: "A vaccine may be due" / "Pode haver uma vacina a tomar"; "Open Lotties to see the vaccine schedule." / "Abra o Lotties para ver o calendário de vacinas."; named "{baby}: {vaccine} may be due according to the schedule." / "{baby}: {vaccine} pode estar na época, segundo o calendário.". Tag `vaccine:{baby}:{doseId}`, click `/vaccines` with the baby selected. A retry rebuilds the dose id from the key.

## 7. Migration `Vaccines` (after `MoreEventTypes`)
Creates `vaccinations`; adds the three baby columns and `baby_notification_preferences.vaccine_reminders`; rebuilds `ix_baby_notification_preferences_baby_id` with the wider filter. No data is changed. Apply with `dotnet ef database update` (docs/DEPLOY.md "Applying migrations").

## 8. Updating the schedule (operator, at least yearly)
1. Check the sources for new editions (PNI calendar pages and technical notes; the SBP and SBIm calendars, usually published mid-year as "2026/2027"). Re-read document 17's "Needs review" list (R-DOC-2, R-DOC-4, R-DOC-5 name things to watch).
2. Edit `code/api/data/vaccine-schedule.br.json` by hand or with a throw-away script that keeps the canonical format (`json.dumps(indent=2, ensure_ascii=False)` plus a trailing newline): change ages and limits, add doses with every schema v2 field, keep `needsReview` and its reason for anything uncertain, update `sources[].edition`/`retrievedOn`, and bump `generatedOn` (it names the ETag; the hash part changes anyway).
3. Never rename or remove a dose id that families may have recorded unless it is truly gone: records keep their `doseId` and `doseLabel`; an id that disappears falls into the History as "not in the schedule". When a dose changes meaning, add a new id.
4. Run `dotnet test code/api/Lotties.sln` (`VaccineScheduleDataTests` validates the file; its pinned counts and examples change with the data, update them) and `npm --prefix code/web run test -- --run src/vaccines`. The vectors use their own small schedules, so they only change when the engine rules change.
5. Update document 17 (sources table, schedule table, disagreements, needs-review list) and deploy the API: clients pick up the new file through the ETag within an hour.

## 9. Licences (owner decision before publishing)
The data comes from public calendars: gov.br content is CC BY-ND 3.0 (with an "all rights reserved" line next to it); the SBP and SBIm calendars are all rights reserved. Lotties ships extracted facts only (which vaccine, at what age, how many doses, which interval), restructured in our own words, with attribution and links (the Sources sheet and `sources[].licenceNote`). We recommend that the owner decides before publishing whether facts-only reuse is enough or permission from SBP and SBIm should be asked (document 17 section 2, R-DOC-1).

## 10. Not covered
Special populations and schedules: premature babies (notice only), immunocompromised children and CRIE schedules, HBsAg-positive mothers, Indigenous children, travel and outbreak doses ("dose zero"), catch-up schemes after missed doses (beyond the SUS latest ages in the data), conditional doses other than the RSV antibody (yellow fever booster after a first dose at 5 years or later, the Hib booster after acellular combinations, the second adolescent ACWY dose: shown with the review marker), product-dependent doses (RV5, Comirnaty, PCV transition: shown with the review marker), and the SBIm adolescent calendar. Document 17 section 8 lists the details.
