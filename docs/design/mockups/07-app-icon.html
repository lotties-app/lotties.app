Baby Routine · design review 07

App icon and wordmark: five concepts

Direction B "Linen and Ink": warm paper tile, ink and terracotta, the illustration set's soft volume lit from the top left. Each concept below is shown at every size, on home screens, under Android masks, as a monochrome badge, in a browser tab, on the sign-in screen and in the top bar, with computed contrast. The ranking and the reasoning are in docs/design/07-app-icon.md.

  1. E Mobile: the strongest idea (baby, day, night, calm) and the most ownable; needs its own favicon.
  2. C br monogram: the best at 16 px and the most editorial; says "brand" more than "baby".
  3. D Moon bassinet: the clearest "baby asleep"; busier, and the bowl can read as a dish.
  4. A Cloud cradle: friendly, close to the illustrations; moon and cloud are common nursery imagery.
  5. B Day disc: the most on-brand with the day ruler, but abstract (an eclipse, a media scrubber) and no baby cue.
