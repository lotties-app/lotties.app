How these numbers are calculated
A day
Runs from 06:00 to 06:00 (Lia's usual wake-up hour), so each day holds its naps and the night that follows. Today is left out of averages until it ends.
Night sleep
From bedtime to wake-up, minus the time awake during night wakings.
Longest stretch
The longest part of the night without a logged waking.
Partly logged days
Days without a finished night, without naps, or with a gap of more than 5 hours in the daytime log. They are drawn with stripes and left out of averages and comparisons.
Comparisons
Only with Lia's own previous period, and only when both periods have at least 4 fully logged days. Differences under 15 minutes are called "about the same".
