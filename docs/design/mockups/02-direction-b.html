B · Linen & Ink

A calm notebook on the nightstand: warm paper, quiet ink and one terracotta pencil. Light-first and editorial, with a serif for the numbers that matter; each day reads like a page you are glad you kept. Its dark side is warm ink, not blue night, so it is gentler at 3 a.m. by construction.

  • Type: serif accent for names, hero numbers, times and amounts (system stack Iowan / Palatino / Georgia here; ship Fraunces or Newsreader, SIL OFL 1.1); system sans for everything else.
  • Icons: monoline 1.5px outline (Tabler Icons, MIT), in 12px-radius "stamps" instead of circles.
  • Shape: 20px cards with a 1px paper edge in light, 16px tiles, hairline rules between rows, small-caps section labels with a rule.
  • Signature: the day ruler (awake window as an ink line, predicted window as a soft band), a cut-paper sun and hills, 5-7% paper grain.
  • Primary action terracotta; selection is solid ink (inverts in dark). Ink blue reserved for links and charts.
  • Night feed: amber on near-black, no grain, text at ~45% of normal luminance, blue channel at most 0x5e.
