# Doctor dashboard: the sections a family shares with each doctor, her own dashboard and patient switcher (design, 2026-10-22)

Owner feedback after using doctor access (docs/design/16-doctor-access.md): a doctor saw the parents' whole app, including data she should not have, and the experience was not built for a clinician. She needs her own dashboard and an easy way to move between patients. Decided:

1. The Owner chooses, per doctor, which **sections** she sees; the server enforces them on every route.
2. A doctor may follow dozens of patients: her device **does not delta-sync** their event history like a parent's; the dashboard reads what a screen shows from scope-filtered server endpoints with a short cache.
3. A separate **DoctorShell** (`/doctor`) with its own navigation, a Patients home, a clinical Summary, a read-only Timeline, Growth, Medications and Notes, and a **patient switcher**.

Appointments are never a doctor's (a separate change refuses them to the Doctor role); nothing here shares or shows them.

## 1. Sections ("doctor scopes")

| Code | Section (en / pt-BR) | What it shares | Routes it opens to a Doctor |
|---|---|---|---|
| `daily_log` | Daily log / Diário | Events (sleep, feeds, diapers, symptoms, temperatures, ...), predictions and patterns, the timeline, the event types | `GET /event-types`, `GET /events`, `GET /events/{id}`, `GET /predictions`, `GET /timeline` |
| `growth` | Growth / Crescimento | Measurements, her consultation measurements | `GET /measurements`, `PUT`/`DELETE /measurements/{id}` |
| `medications` | Medications / Medicamentos | Medications, prescriptions and status, doses given (in the summary), her proposals and their status | `GET /medications`, `GET /prescriptions`, `GET /medications/status`, `GET /prescription-proposals`, `GET`/`PUT`/`DELETE /prescription-proposals/{id}` |
| `vaccines` | Vaccines / Vacinas | Vaccination records and the status engine (due, overdue) | `GET /vaccinations` |
| `formulas` | Formulas / Fórmulas | Formulas in use and their history | `GET /formulas` |
| `notes` | Notes / Notas | Her notes and recommendations, the shared notes | `GET /notes`, `GET`/`PUT`/`DELETE /notes/{id}`, `PUT /notes/{id}/read`, `GET /note-reads` |

- A new doctor gets all six (the invite form starts with every switch on). The Owner can turn any off at invite time and later in Hub > Family > "Doctor access"; with none, she sees only the baby's name and age.
- **Never a doctor's**, whatever is shared: appointments, members and the care providers directory (`BabyReadFamily`: any member but a Doctor, docs/design/16-doctor-access.md section 11, docs/design/22-care-providers.md), invites, the access log, reminder preferences and baby reminders, push, the Napper import (and any export), other babies of the family.
- Open to a Doctor without a section: `GET /babies/{id}` (name, birth date), `DELETE /members/me` (leave), `GET /clinical-summary` (it filters its own sections), `GET /doctor/patients`.
- Scopes apply only to the Doctor role. Owner, Parent, Caregiver and Viewer pass every scope policy.

## 2. Data model and migration
- Domain `Babies/DoctorScope.cs` (enum and codes) and `Babies/DoctorScopeSet.cs` (immutable set: `All`, `None`, `Parse` (unknown or null code 400 `validation_failed`, field `doctorScopes`; duplicates collapse; an empty list is allowed), `Contains`, `Codes` in canonical order, `ToStorage`/`FromStorage` (lenient: an unknown stored code is ignored)).
- `BabyMember.DoctorScopes` (null = all six), `EffectiveDoctorScopes`, `Sees(scope)` (true for every non-Doctor role). `Invite.DoctorScopes` (only with role Doctor, else 400), copied onto the member by `Baby.AdmitWith`. `Baby.ChangeDoctorScopes(userId, scopes)` (the member must be a Doctor). A member who stops being a Doctor loses her scopes.
- Migration `DoctorScopes` (after `CareProviders`): `baby_members.doctor_scopes` and `invites.doctor_scopes`, varchar(128), nullable, comma-separated codes; no row changes (NULL = all six, so existing doctors keep what they had).

## 3. Enforcement (server)
- `Api/Authorization/BabyScopeRequirement` + `BabyScopeHandler`: from the `{babyId}` route value through `IBabyAccessService` (the membership is loaded once per request). Policies `BabyScope:daily_log`, `BabyScope:growth`, `BabyScope:medications`, `BabyScope:vaccines`, `BabyScope:formulas` and `BabyScope:notes`, added on top of the role policies of the routes in section 1. A Doctor without the section gets **403**, the same answer as an insufficient role (routes are refused as a whole; the per-item 404 of private notes is unchanged inside a shared section).
- Memberships are read from the database on every request: a change of sections applies to the doctor's next request.
- **Fields of one section inside another's answer** (2026-10-23): `Domain/Babies/SectionView` (a Doctor's effective sections, everything for any other role) decides what a shared route still leaves out. The vaccine profile on the baby (`vaccineProfile`, `gestationalAgeWeeks`, `rsvMaternalVaccination`) is `null` without `vaccines`; medication doses, logged as built-in `medication` events, are left out of the events, the timeline, the predictions and the patient card's last entry without `medications` (`GET /events/{id}` of one is 404); the formula a bottle names (`formula_id`, `formula_name`) is removed from feeding payloads and the summary's `feeding.formulas` is empty without `formulas`. Tests: `Lotties.Domain.Tests/Babies/SectionViewTests.cs`, `Lotties.Api.Tests/Endpoints/DoctorSectionFieldsEndpointTests.cs` (omitted without the section, present with it, every family role unaffected). The web keeps no vaccine profile for such a patient (`fromBabyDto` ignores the `null`s, and the pull replaces the stored baby).
- `EndpointRoleMatrixTests` keeps the role decision of every route and adds a **scope table**: every route the Doctor may call per the role matrix is either in the table (with its section) or in an explicit, commented "open to every doctor" set, so a new route cannot ship without a decision. For every scoped route a Doctor without that section is refused and a Doctor with only that section passes; a Doctor never reaches members, invites, the access log, reminder preferences or the import.

## 4. Endpoints for the dashboard
- `GET /doctor/patients?timeZone=&utcOffsetMinutes=` (any signed-in user; the babies where she is the Doctor): per patient `{babyId, name, birthDate, doctorScopes, lastEventAt, week, latestWeight, pendingProposals, vaccinesDue, sharedNotes}`, each field `null` without its section. `week` = the 7 local days ending today, averaged like the parents' Insights (sleep minutes, feeds, bottle ml, diapers per day). It records an access-log view for every listed patient.
- `GET /babies/{id}/clinical-summary?from=&to=&timeZone=&utcOffsetMinutes=` (local days, inclusive, at most 92): sleep, feeding, diapers, symptoms and temperatures (daily_log), medications (doses given per medication, active prescriptions with provenance), vaccines (counts and due doses from the status engine), growth (measurements), formulas (current and history); a section is `null` without its scope. Shapes in docs/SPEC.md "Doctor sections and dashboard".
- `GET /babies/{id}/timeline?from=&to=` (daily_log): the non-deleted events overlapping the instants, at most 8 days (the client asks one local day and the evening before).
- `PATCH /members/{userId} {role?, doctorScopes?}` and `POST /invites {..., doctorScopes?}` (Owner); `BabyDto`/`MemberDto`/`InviteDto.doctorScopes` (effective codes for a Doctor, `null` otherwise).
- **Server-side numbers, the parents' rules** (pure Domain code, `Domain/Clinical`): local-midnight days from `LocalClock` (23 or 25 hours across DST); sleep sessions = sleep events whose kind is absent, `nap` or `night` (markers and night wakings are not sleep), effective time per day = clipped length minus the pauses spread over the event, a running sleep counted until now; averages over the days with data, today left out unless it is the only one; bedtime and wake-up medians as `bedtimeOf`/`wakeOf`/`usualClock` of the web; feeds by the day they started, gaps over 8 hours left out of the interval; bottle ml over the days with an amount; diapers wet = WET + MIXED, dirty = DIRTY + MIXED; doses from the medication events. Golden tests cover a sleep across midnight, DST days (America/New_York 2026-03-08 and 2026-11-01), pauses, running sleep, today's exclusion, medians across midnight, feed gaps, formula grouping and mixed diapers.

## 5. The doctor's device: lean data
- `sync/pull.ts` `pullAll` sends every Doctor membership to `doctor/doctorSync.ts` `pullDoctorPatient` instead of the parents' pull: it first forgets what a patient must not keep (`forgetUnsharedPatientData`: always the events, event types, formulas, appointments, vaccinations and note reads with their cursors; the rows, cursors and queued changes of every section no longer shared), then pulls only what she works on offline: notes (`notes`), the medication list, prescriptions and her proposals (`medications`, for Prescribe and "Propose a change"), the latest weight (`growth`, for per-kg doses). A revoked section is purged on the first pull that no longer lists it; the dashboard also drops its cached answers for that patient at once and asks for a sync when the server's patient cards show other sections than the device.
- Everything else is read on demand with TanStack Query (`doctor/useDoctorData.ts`; fresh for a minute, the last answer kept while offline): the patient cards, the summary of a period, one day of the timeline, the event types.
- The parents' shell never shows a patient: `SelectedBabyProvider` lists only the user's own babies (every role but Doctor), so Now, Day, Insights, the Hub and its pages, the running-timer pill and reminders are never about a patient (the Hub shows a doctor only a Patients row that opens `/doctor`).

## 6. Dashboard (web, `code/web/src/doctor/`)
- **Landing and mode.** A user whose memberships are all Doctor lands on `/doctor`. A user who is both a parent and a doctor gets "My babies" / "My patients" in the account menu; the choice is remembered on the device and `/` follows it. `/patients`, `/proposals`, `/notes` and `/from-doctor` send a doctor without a baby of her own to the dashboard.
- **Shell.** `DoctorShell` (header with the patient switcher on a patient, else "My patients"; sync icon; account menu with the doctor's own Settings at `/doctor/settings`), `DoctorNav` (Patients | Summary | Timeline | Growth | Meds | Notes: a bottom pill on phones, a left rail on wide screens; no add button). Routes: `/doctor`, `/doctor/settings`, `/doctor/:babyId` (Summary), `/timeline`, `/growth`, `/medications`, `/notes`.
- **Patients home.** One card per patient: name, clinical age, the last log ("Last log 3 hours ago"; "No logs in 4 days" as a calm informational flag after three quiet days), the last seven days per day (sleep, feeds and bottle ml, diapers), the latest weight and its date, and notices ("Your proposal is pending", "Vaccines due: 2"); each line only for its shared section. Search, sort by recent activity, name or age (the sort is also the switcher's order). An empty state explains how an invite works.
- **Summary.** Period chips (Last 7 days, 14 days, 30 days, Since last note, Custom up to 92 days; kept in the URL), then the sections of section 4 in cards, each number with a plain "how it is computed" note; "Not shared with you" for a section the family keeps, "No data in this period." for a shared one without data. Descriptive only: no fever colouring, no verdicts, no percentile curves. The page ends with "Stop following Lia" (her own `DELETE /members/me`, after the usual unsynced-changes warning; the patient then leaves the device), since a doctor never sees the family's Members.
- **Timeline.** The Day screen's date bar, summary strip, ribbon, filter and rows, read-only (`?day=yyyy-mm-dd`), fetched per day.
- **Growth, Medications, Notes** reuse the existing screens (consultation measurements; active prescriptions with "Prescribed by" and My proposals with Prescribe; notes with the composer), each behind its section.

## 7. Patient switcher
- In the header: the patient's avatar, name and clinical age (and "2 of 12" on wide screens). A tap opens a popover with a search combobox (`aria-activedescendant`, arrows, Home/End, Enter), the recently opened patients (up to five, newest first) and all patients in the Patients sort order; the current one is checked.
- Previous and next chevrons beside the name, and the `[` / `]` keys (not while typing or with a dialog open), move through the patients in the sort order, wrapping around.
- Switching keeps the section ("Growth" stays "Growth"); the URL always carries the patient id (bookmarks and the back button work). The device remembers the last opened patient (the navigation's target from the Patients home), the last section of each patient (where its card opens) and the recents (`doctor/dashboardStorage.ts`, ids only, cleared with the account's device state).

## 8. Privacy (LGPD)
- The legal basis stays the parent's specific consent at invite time, now **per section**: the invite lists what the doctor will see and the consent line says so ("The doctor who accepts will see the sections you choose for Lia ..."). Minimisation: a doctor reads only the sections shared with her, only per baby; members, appointments and settings never. The Owner can narrow or widen the sections at any time; the change applies to the doctor's next request and her device forgets a revoked section on its next sync.
- Transparency: the access log keeps one entry per hour per doctor and baby, now also for the summary, the timeline and the patient list (one entry per listed patient); its header names what each doctor sees. The doctor reads "You see only what the family shared." on her Patients and Summary, and "Shared with you: ..." when she joins.
- Not done (recorded): a per-section consent record with its timestamp (the access log shows reads, not changes of sections), an audit of section changes, access log retention.

## 9. Not in v1
Percentile curves (the WHO growth standards tables are CC BY-NC-SA, not compatible with shipping them here; a licensed source or the parents' own data is future work), appointments for doctors, exports or a printable/PDF summary for a visit, doctor-to-parent chat, per-item sharing (a single note or a date range), push to the doctor.
