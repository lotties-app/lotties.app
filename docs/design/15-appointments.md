# Appointments: a per-baby calendar of doctor visits, vaccines and exams (design, 2026-10-22)

Owner request: keep the baby's appointments (consultations, vaccines, exams) with reminders before each one, see them in a month calendar, add them to the phone's calendar, and note how a visit went. The app keeps what the family entered; it never suggests a visit, a schedule or a vaccine.

## 1. Data model (per baby, synced offline-first like formulas)
- `Appointment` (Domain `Appointments/Appointment.cs`, table `appointments`, migration `Appointments`): `id` (client GUID), `babyId`, `title` (required, 1-80), `type` (`consultation` | `vaccine` | `exam` | `other`), `startsAt` (UTC instant, 2000-01-01..2100-12-31; may be in the past to log a visit after the fact), `durationMinutes` (optional 5-480), `location` (optional, 200), `doctorName` (optional, 80), `notes` (optional, 2000), `outcome` (optional, 2000, how the visit went), `status` (`upcoming` | `done` | `cancelled`), `reminderOffsetsMinutes` (any of 10080, 1440, 120, 30, each once, stored largest first; absent on a write = `[1440, 120]`, `[]` = none), `createdBy`, `createdAt`, `updatedAt` (server receive time, the delta cursor), `clientUpdatedAt` (client edit time, decides conflicts). Text is trimmed, NUL rejected (400 `validation_failed`, `field`).
- Never deleted: `cancelled` is the soft delete (kept, synced, listed, never reminded; restorable). Any status may change to any other with a full-state write, except that `done` needs a start that is not in the future: `startsAt <= server now + 5 min` (the client-clock allowance of every edit time), else 422 `appointment_not_started` (`field: status`). Moving a done visit into the future is refused the same way.
- Storage: type and status as their lower-case codes, the offsets as `1440,120` (`reminder_offsets_minutes`, varchar 20). Indexes `(baby_id, starts_at)` (calendar range), `(baby_id, updated_at)` (delta), partial `ix_appointments_upcoming` `(baby_id, starts_at) WHERE status = 'upcoming'` (scheduler look-ahead). FK to the baby and the creator, restrict.

## 2. API (`/api/v1/babies/{babyId}/appointments`)
- `GET ?since=&from=&to=` (any member): the baby's appointments, cancelled and done included. `since` = the delta (`updatedAt >= since`, ordered by `(updatedAt, id)`); `from`/`to` = the calendar range (`from <= startsAt < to`, ordered by `(startsAt, id)` without `since`; both combine). `to` must be after `from` and at most 400 days later (400 `validation_failed`, `field: to`). No paging (a family has few).
- `GET /{appointmentId}` (any member): one; unknown or of another baby 404 `appointment_not_found`.
- `PUT /{appointmentId}` (Owner, Parent): full state `{title, type, startsAt, durationMinutes?, location?, doctorName?, notes?, outcome?, status?, reminderOffsetsMinutes?, updatedAt?}`; 201 + `Location` / 200; idempotent replay (nothing changes, not even `updatedAt`), a stale client time is ignored (200, stored copy), an id of another baby 409 `appointment_baby_mismatch`. Create, edit, mark done, cancel and restore are all this route. No `DELETE` (405).
- Roles (EndpointRoleMatrixTests): both `GET` Read (Owner, Parent, Caregiver, Viewer), `PUT` Write (Owner, Parent). A future Doctor role is read-only (not implemented here).

## 3. Reminders (push kind `appointment_reminder`; docs/design/09-push-notifications.md section 2.9)
- Per member and baby preference `appointment_reminders` (`baby_notification_preferences.appointment_reminders`, NOT NULL DEFAULT TRUE): on by default, because the family created the appointment on purpose; the device opt-in (browser permission + subscription) is still needed. Members who never stored preferences are candidates with the default while the baby has an upcoming appointment within a week; Viewers never receive (and are shown the switch off).
- For every upcoming appointment that has not started and each of its offsets: sent at `start - offset` to every Owner, Parent and Caregiver with the preference on and an opted-in device. A send time inside the member's quiet hours (read in their zone, daylight saving applied) is delayed to the end of the quiet period when that is still before the start, else that reminder is skipped. An offset whose send time was already past when the appointment was last changed (created, moved, restored) is skipped. Of the offsets due at one tick only the latest goes (an earlier unsent one is superseded, so two reminders never arrive together). Done and cancelled appointments never remind.
- Key `appt:{appointmentId:N}:{start utc yyyyMMddHHmm}:{offset}`: editing the start plans new reminders. Stale cutoff 30 minutes; time to live until the start; urgency normal; snooze and dismiss as for other kinds (no buttons).
- Payload codes only: `{v, id, kind: "appointment_reminder", babyId, apptId, offset, at (the start), lang, token, names?: {baby}}`; never the title, the doctor or the notes. A retry rebuilds `apptId`, `at` and `offset` from the key.
- Lock-screen text (the worker words it in the device zone): "Appointment reminder / Appointment tomorrow at 15:30." (pt-BR "Lembrete de consulta / Consulta amanhã às 15:30."), "in a week, on Thursday, October 22 at ..." ("daqui a uma semana, quinta-feira, 22 de outubro, às ..."), "in 2 hours, at ..." ("daqui a 2 horas, às ..."), "in 30 minutes, at ..." ("daqui a 30 minutos, às ..."); when the offset's phrase is no longer true (delayed by quiet hours) the calendar relation: "today at", "tomorrow at", "on {date} at" ("hoje às", "amanhã às", "marcada para {date}, às"). With show-names: "Lia: appointment tomorrow at 15:30." Device tag `appt:{appointment}` (the next reminder replaces the earlier one). A click opens `/appointments#notify=appt&baby=..&appt=..&d=..`: the baby is selected, a short sync runs, and the calendar opens that appointment's detail.

## 4. Web
- Dexie schema v5 table `appointments` (`id, babyId, [babyId+startsAt], [babyId+updatedAt]`), pulled after formulas and before events (`pullAppointments`, cursor `appointments`), pushed through the outbox (`entityType: 'appointment'`, no dependency), merged by client edit time, purged with the baby. Commands in `db/appointmentCommands.ts` mirror the API rules (title, duration, "done only once started") and queue the full state; a later edit absorbs a queued one.
- Route `/appointments` (not in the bottom bar), reached from the "Next appointment" card on Now (the soonest upcoming appointment that has not ended, starting within 14 days; tap opens its detail), the calendar button beside the filter in the Day header, Settings > Appointments, and a reminder.
- Month calendar (`MonthCalendar.tsx`): the month's days in local time, the week starting as the language's region does (the device region when it speaks the app language: an English user in the UK starts on Monday), today ringed, up to three dots for the day's appointments (cancelled ones left out), the chosen day filled; a table with role `grid`, weekday column headers, each day a button labelled "Saturday, October 24, 2 appointments"; arrows, Home/End and Page Up/Down move (crossing months), Enter or Space chooses; choosing the chosen day again clears the filter.
- Agenda: upcoming (not ended) soonest first, then "Past" newest first with chips: Done, Cancelled (struck through) and "Did it happen?" for an upcoming visit whose time has passed; a chosen day lists only its appointments with "Show all appointments". Empty state with "New appointment" for writers.
- Sheet (`AppointmentSheet.tsx`): title, type chips with icons (stethoscope, vaccine, microscope, calendar), date (the app's date field) and time (the native time picker), optional duration stepper (5-480 min), place, doctor, notes, the outcome when the visit is done, reminder chips 1 week / 1 day / 2 hours / 30 min (new: 1 day and 2 hours). A new one starts on the chosen day (else today) at the next whole hour (other days 09:00).
- Detail (`AppointmentDetailSheet.tsx`): when (with "in 3 days" while ahead), place, doctor, reminders, notes, outcome; "Add to calendar" for everyone (an `.ics` file, below); Owners and Parents also Edit, Cancel appointment (Undo in the snackbar) or Restore, and, after the start of a still upcoming visit, the "Did it happen?" card with the optional outcome, "Add measurements" (Insights, Growth) and "Mark as done". Caregivers and Viewers see everything with "You can see the appointments but not change them." and no buttons they cannot use.
- Day: the day's appointments as a compact strip above the ribbon (time, title, status); tap opens the detail in place.
- Settings > Notifications, per baby: "Appointment reminders" switch (calendar icon), on by default.

## 5. "Add to calendar" (`appointments/ics.ts`, pure)
RFC 5545: `VCALENDAR` (`VERSION:2.0`, `PRODID:-//Lotties//Appointments//EN`, `CALSCALE:GREGORIAN`, `METHOD:PUBLISH`) with one `VEVENT`: `UID:{id}@lotties.app`, `DTSTAMP` (export time), `DTSTART`/`DTEND` in UTC basic format (`DTEND` = start + duration, or + 1 hour), `SUMMARY`, `LOCATION`, `DESCRIPTION` ("Doctor: ..." and the notes), `STATUS` (`CANCELLED` or `CONFIRMED`), `LAST-MODIFIED`, one `VALARM` (`ACTION:DISPLAY`, `TRIGGER:-PT{offset}M`) per reminder offset. TEXT escaping of backslash, `;`, `,` and line breaks; CRLF line endings; lines folded at 75 octets of UTF-8, never inside a character. Saved through a Blob link (`downloadIcs.ts`, `lotties-appointment-{local date}.ics`); in the Capacitor shell this should hand the file to the share sheet (TODO in the code).

## 6. Time zones
All instants are UTC; the device zone only shows them. The calendar maths (`appointments/calendarModel.ts`) read local dates and times through `Intl` (or an explicit zone in tests), turn a local date and time into an instant without adding hours (a skipped time lands just after the gap, a repeated one is its first occurrence) and build month grids from calendar dates alone, so daylight saving changes never shift a cell or a day group (tests with Europe/Lisbon and America/New_York). The planner reads quiet hours in the recipient's zone at the reminder's own instant.
