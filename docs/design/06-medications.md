# Medications: prescriptions, safe intervals, shared catalog (design, 2026-10-21)

Owner request: record what the doctor prescribed (e.g. "2 drops of X every morning", "1 drop per kg of Y when the baby has belly pain", "X and Y no closer than 8 hours apart: given at 10:00, next allowed 18:00"), compute the next allowed time, suggest information from other parents using the same medication, let parents share one catalog entry instead of creating duplicates, and know that different brands can be the same active ingredient (Flygas = Luftal = simethicone).

## 0. Guardrails (apply to every feature below)
- The app is a log and reminder tool, not a medical device and never gives dosing advice. The doctor's prescription, entered by the parent, is the only source for a dose or interval used in calculations. UI copy says so where doses or intervals appear.
- "Same active ingredient" is shown as information only ("Contains the same active ingredient: simethicone. Same ingredient, strength not known: Luftal. Ask your doctor or pharmacist before substituting.", in labelled groups by strength and form, docs/design/13-anvisa-catalog.md section 12) and never auto-substitutes.
- Dose-per-kg is a calculator over the doctor's rule and the baby's latest weight: it shows its inputs (rule, weight, date of the weight, rounding) and always requires the parent to confirm; a stale weight (> 30 days) shows a warning.
- Logging earlier than the allowed time is never blocked: the app asks "Too early: next allowed at 18:00. Log anyway?" and records that it was overridden.
- Community statistics (M3) are opt-in, k-anonymous, aggregated, never per-baby, never phrased as a recommendation, and need an explicit review by the owner before shipping; default OFF.

## 1. Data model
Instance-wide (shared by all families on the same deployment):
- `substances`: id, key (e.g. `simethicone`), names (en, pt-BR), created_at. Seeded with a small curated list; generic names only, no dosing information.
- `products` (the shared catalog: brand/presentation): id, name, normalized_name, form (drops, syrup, tablet, powder, spray, ointment, suppository, inhaler, other; see "Forms and dose units" below), country (ISO), status (`seed` | `community` | `verified`), created_by (nullable user id, never shown), merged_into (nullable), created_at; table `product_substances` (product_id, substance_id, strength_amount, strength_unit, per_amount, per_unit) for e.g. 75 mg per 1 mL. Unique (normalized_name, form, country) for non-merged rows.
- Normalisation for dedupe: lowercase, strip diacritics, drop spaces/punctuation, collapse repeated letters ('flygass' ~ 'flygas'), trigram/edit-distance suggestions (reuse the importer's name matching in Domain).
Per baby:
- `medications` (existing baby catalog): add nullable `product_id` and keep baby-specific name/default dosage/isUsual (the baby's own nickname stays valid).
- `prescriptions`: id (client GUID), baby_id, medication_id, status (`active` | `stopped`), kind (`scheduled` | `as_needed`), dose_amount, dose_unit, dose_per_kg (bool; when true dose_amount is per kg), rounding (`none` | `0.5` | `1` | `0.1`...), schedule for scheduled: `times_of_day` (list of HH:MM), `every_minutes` or `day_parts` (part of the day, see section 2.1, with optional `day_part_reminders`); for as_needed: `min_interval_minutes`, `max_doses_per_24h` (nullable), `indication` (free text, e.g. "belly pain"); `interval_group` (nullable string: all prescriptions with the same group share the interval clock: a dose of any member starts the interval for all, e.g. "colic"); `start_date`, `end_date` (nullable), `prescriber` (text), `instructions` (text), created_by, created_at, updated_at, deleted_at (soft delete for sync).
- Forms and dose units (2026-10-21): the dose unit stays free text (what the doctor wrote: drops/gotas, ml, mg, anything else). Four forms are dosed in counted units, recognised in en and pt-BR, singular or plural, any case, with or without accents (`Domain/Medications/DoseUnits`, mirrored by `code/web/src/medications/doseUnits.ts`): `spray` (nasal or oral spray, e.g. saline sprays) -> sprays (pt-BR "jatos"; "borrifadas" also recognised), `inhaler` ("Bombinha") -> puffs (pt-BR "jatos"; "inalações" also recognised), `suppository` -> suppositories, `ointment` (ointment or cream, topical; pt-BR "Pomada/creme") -> applications. A dose in a counted unit is never per kg (400 `dose.perKg`, the editor shows the same rule), sprays and puffs are whole numbers and suppositories are multiples of 0.5 (real prescriptions use half suppositories; 400 `dose.amount`, `DoseUnits.AmountStepOf`, web `amountStepOf`); applications accept any amount. The editor offers only the counted unit's chip when the medication is linked to a product of one of these forms (drops/ml/mg otherwise); amounts read with plurals ("1 spray", "2 sprays", "2 jatos", "1 supositório"). Logged doses (event payloads) are not checked: they record what was given. No schema change: the form is stored as text.
- Who logs doses (2026-10-22): Owners, Parents and Caregivers log a dose from an existing prescription with the same early-dose confirmation and recorded `override` as everyone; only Owners and Parents create, edit or delete prescriptions, medications and catalog links (a Caregiver sees them read-only, without "Add prescription" in the Meds tray). A dose shows who gave it ("Given by Ana") on the Day row and in the read-only sheet when someone else logged it (docs/SPEC.md "Roles and permissions").
- Dose events stay generic `medication` events: payload `medications: [{ medicationId, name, dose?: {amount, unit}, prescriptionId?, kgUsed?, override?: true }]`.
- Provenance (2026-10-22; docs/design/16-doctor-access.md section 10): a baby's doctor may suggest a prescription (`prescription_proposals`, same `DoseRule` and `PrescriptionSchedule` rules and validation, never active, never reminding); when an Owner or Parent accepts it, the prescription is created from it with `prescribed_by_user_id`, `prescribed_by_name` (her name at that moment), `proposal_id`, `original_snapshot` (jsonb, what she sent) and `prescriber` = her name. A parent may still change it: any content change (not stopping or resuming) sets `changed_by_parent` for good and the original stays readable ("See what Dr. Ana prescribed"). A proposed change (`replaces_prescription_id`) ends the current prescription on the accept day (stopped, history kept). `instructions` is up to 2000 characters (was 1000) so the doctor's text is carried verbatim. The guardrails of section 0 are unchanged: the dose still comes only from the prescription the family accepted, the per-kg confirmation and the early-dose question stay, and no copy implies the app checked or recommends the doctor's dose.

## 2. Rules (pure Domain, offline-capable on the web too)
`MedicationStatusCalculator(prescriptions, doseEvents, now, weight)` returns per prescription: `availableNow`, `availableAt`, `nextScheduledAt`, `overdueBy`, `dosesLast24h`, `limitReached`, `courseEnded`, `suggestedDose` (when dose_per_kg and weight known: amount x kg rounded, with the inputs). Interval = min over prescription interval and group interval from the latest dose of any member; scheduled prescriptions: next time-of-day not yet logged today (a logged dose within +-90 minutes of a scheduled time counts as that slot). Property tests on timezone handling (local times of day) and midnight.

### 2.1 Part of the day (2026-10-22)
Owner request: prescriptions such as "1 drop in the morning" or "1 pill in the morning" where the clock time does not matter.
- Schedule: a third scheduled shape beside "Times of day" and "Every few hours": `dayParts` = 1-4 of `morning`, `afternoon`, `evening`, `night` (stored `day_parts`, e.g. `morning,night`; kept in day order). One dose per chosen part per day. Old prescriptions are unchanged (nullable columns, migration `PrescriptionDayParts`).
- Windows (fixed constants, one place each side: `Domain/Prescriptions/DayPartWindows`, web `medications/dayParts.ts`, identical by the shared vectors `code/web/src/medications/dayPartVectors.json` read by both test suites): morning 05:00-12:00, afternoon 12:00-18:00, evening 18:00-22:00, night 22:00-05:00, in the baby's local time (the family's UTC offset at the instant, so DST moves the windows with the wall clock). A routine day starts at 05:00: the night from 22:00 to 05:00 belongs to the day it started on (at 02:00 "today's parts" are still yesterday's). pt-BR names: Manhã, Tarde, Noite (evening), Madrugada (night); keys stay `morning/afternoon/evening/night`.
- Status (pure, C# and TS identical): per chosen part of the routine day: `given` (an own dose of the medication logged inside the window; the first dose time is shown, the count kept), `due` (window open, no dose), `upcoming` (not started), `missed` (ended without a dose; information only, neutral wording "Not logged", never an alarm, no automatic action). A part that ended before the prescription was created is left out unless it has a dose. Overall: the current part, the next part due or upcoming (today's or the next routine day's), "1 of 2 today". The course dates compare with the routine day (the last night of a course runs until 05:00 the next date); interval groups and the 24 h rules apply as for times of day (a part prescription has no own interval).
- Guardrails: logging when the current part already has a dose asks "Already given this morning at 08:12. Log another dose anyway?"; logging outside every chosen part asks "Not one of the prescribed parts of the day (Morning). Log anyway?" (added: without it a second morning dose given in the afternoon would pass silently). Both reuse the early-dose confirmation and record `override: true`. Nothing suggests changing a dose; as-needed rules are unchanged.
- Meds tray / Now: the card lists today's parts ("Morning · Given at 08:12", "Night · Due until 05:00", "Afternoon · Not logged"), the state line reads "Morning: due until 12:00 · 0 of 2 today" or "Next: Night from 22:00 · 1 of 2 today", and the Log button reads "Give now" while the current part is due (one tap with the prescribed dose; per-kg doses keep the confirm step).
- Reminders: optional per prescription, off by default (`dayPartReminders`, one `HH:mm` per chosen part inside its window; defaults 08:00, 14:00, 19:00 and 22:00; the owner's 21:00 for night was moved to 22:00 because 21:00 lies in the evening window). The planner sends `dose_due` at that time only while the part has no dose (see docs/design/09-push-notifications.md 2.1). Without reminders the editor does not offer the push prompt after saving.

## 3. Features and flows
1. Prescription editor (Settings > Medications, and from the Meds tray): pick/create the medication (shared catalog search first), dose (+ per kg toggle), kind scheduled/as needed, interval, max per day, indication, dates, prescriber, notes; plain-language summary preview ("Give 1 drop per kg, no more often than every 8 hours, when she has belly pain").
2. Now screen: a "Meds" card/tile showing for each active prescription its state (e.g. "Available at 18:00", "Due now", "2 of 3 doses today"), the Meds tray lists them with availability, logs with the prescribed dose prefilled and the suggested per-kg dose, asks confirmation when early (override recorded). Day rows show the dose and prescription name.
3. Shared catalog search when adding a medication: type a name, matches from the shared catalog appear (fuzzy, substance names and aliases included); choosing one links the baby's medication to the product (no duplicate rows); "Not listed? Add it" creates a `community` product after the dedupe check; the same-active-ingredient note shows the sibling products.
4. Napper import: map medicine comment tokens to shared products/substances (reuses the importer's token matching; suggestions prefer catalog matches).
5. Reminders are out of scope for M1 (no push); the Now card and Day are the surface.

## 4. Community suggestions (M3, design only now)
Aggregates per product and age band computed server-side from babies who opted in: number of babies (show only when >= 20 and from >= 5 families), IQR of logged dose, IQR of intervals, common indications; displayed as "Parents using this report ..." with the disclaimer and a "Not medical advice" label, never prefilling a dose. Contribution toggle per user/baby (default OFF). Cross-instance sharing needs a separate community catalog service with moderation: out of scope until the owner decides.

## 5. Data sources and licensing for seeds
Official list (2026-10-22): brands, forms and active ingredients of Brazil's registered medicines are imported from ANVISA's open data by an admin tool, as `official` products; see docs/design/13-anvisa-catalog.md (licence, mapping, caveats). Do not invent medical facts. Seed substances (generic names, en and pt-BR) and brand to substance mappings only when verifiable from an authoritative public source (ANVISA bulario/consulta de medicamentos, CMED list, national formularies) and cite the source URL in the seed file; skip anything unverifiable (the owner's examples: Flygas and Luftal are simethicone products; Colicaliv, Puravit, Colidis: verify before seeding). Avoid WHO ATC (licence). No dosing data in seeds.

## 6. Waves
M1 (API): entities + migrations + endpoints + status calculator + tests; seed substances/products with citations. M2 (web): prescription editor, shared search/dedupe, Now/Meds tray/Day integration, offline status calculation mirroring the API, i18n. M3: Napper import mapping to catalog; community aggregates (needs owner review); medication-related prediction (PRN timing vs belly pain episodes) as an ML benchmark target.
